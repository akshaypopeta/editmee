About Sign PDF Tool

Sign PDF is a fast and secure online tool that allows you to add electronic signatures to PDF documents directly from your web browser. Whether you need to sign contracts, agreements, invoices, application forms, business documents, or personal paperwork, this tool makes the process simple and convenient without requiring any software installation.

PDF documents are widely used for legal agreements, employment contracts, business proposals, government forms, financial records, and official paperwork. Instead of printing, signing, and scanning documents manually, you can quickly add your signature digitally and download the signed PDF within seconds.

Our Sign PDF tool supports typed signatures, drawn signatures, and uploaded signature images, allowing you to choose the method that works best for your needs.

Businesses, freelancers, students, and professionals frequently use electronic signatures to streamline document workflows, reduce paperwork, and improve productivity. Digital signing helps save time while maintaining a professional document appearance.

If your document requires additional security after signing, you can use our Protect PDF tool to add password protection before sharing the file.

Our PDF signing tool is designed for speed, simplicity, and security. Simply upload your PDF, place your signature where needed, and download the signed document instantly.

Many organizations use electronic signatures to approve contracts, process employee documents, sign invoices, authorize business agreements, and complete administrative paperwork without printing physical copies.

Students often use PDF signatures when submitting applications, academic forms, internship agreements, project approvals, and university documentation.

If your PDF contains multiple pages and you need to reorganize them before signing, you can use our Merge PDF or Split PDF tools.

Security and privacy are important priorities. Documents often contain confidential information such as contracts, agreements, financial statements, employee records, and personal documents. We are committed to protecting your files throughout the signing process.

You can further enhance your signed PDFs using our Watermark PDF and Page Number PDF tools.

Our goal is to provide a reliable, user-friendly, and secure PDF signing experience that helps users complete document workflows faster and more efficiently.

How to Use the Sign PDF Tool

  1. Click the Choose PDF button.
  2. Select the PDF document you want to sign.
  3. Create or upload your signature.
  4. Position the signature on the PDF page.
  5. Click the Sign PDF button.
  6. Download your signed PDF document.
